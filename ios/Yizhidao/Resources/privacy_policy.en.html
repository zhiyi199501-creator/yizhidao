Yiwanjia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-30 · Brand name: 易玩家

This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use 易玩家 (Yiwanjia). Using the app means you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Email: If you sign in with an email code, we collect that address to send the code and identify your account. We do not collect contacts or photos.
  • Sign in with Apple: We receive Apple’s user identifier (and a name or email only if you choose to share them) to create and recognize your account.
  • Sign in with Google: On Android we receive Google’s identifier (and a name or email if provided) for the same purpose.
  • On-device data: Casts, saved readings, and settings stay on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing data removes them.
  • Account on the server: After you sign in, we store the identifier, nickname, and other fields needed to keep you signed in.
  • Purchases: If you buy Unlock Readings, we store Apple’s transaction identifier with your account so we can restore the entitlement. We do not store your questions or reading text for this.
  • AI readings: When you ask for a reading, the question and hexagram data are sent to our servers and to a third-party model.
  • Service logs: Access logs used to debug failures. They do not contain passwords or login codes in clear text.

2. How we use information

  • To provide sign-in, casting, scripture, AI readings, and history;
  • To keep the account and service secure;
  • To generate a reading when you ask for one.

3. Sharing with third parties

  • Email: Login codes are sent by a mail provider (such as Resend). We give them only your address and the code.
  • Apple / Google: They complete sign-in. We receive only the tokens and identifiers needed to finish login.
  • AI readings: Your question and hexagram data go to a model provider (such as OpenAI or DeepSeek). Their privacy policies apply to that processing.
  • Commentary: Some in-app notes come from the 黄庭书院 knowledge base. Copyright stays with the authors. They are for reading inside this app only.

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not use third-party ad tracking and does not request App Tracking Transparency.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable encryption and access control. Servers run in a cloud environment with basic protections. We keep data only as long as the service needs it. After you delete your account, we delete the personal information tied to it.

5. Your rights

  • You can delete the server account in Me → Settings → Delete account, or email us for help;
  • You may ask to see or correct your account information.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their information. If we learn that we have, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Updates

We may revise this policy. Material changes will appear in the app or on this page. Continued use means you accept the revised policy.
